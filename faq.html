Got questions? We've got answers.

Are the games completely free to play?

Yes, all the games on our platform are 100% free. You can play as much as you want without any hidden charges or subscriptions.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No downloads or installations are required. All our games are built with HTML5, meaning you can play them instantly directly in your web browser.

Can I play on my mobile phone or tablet?

Absolutely! Our platform and games are fully responsive and optimized for both desktop and mobile devices so you can game on the go.

How often do you add new games?

We update our library regularly. New and exciting HTML5 games are added every week, so there is always something fresh to play.

Do I need to create an account to play?

No, you can jump right in and play as a guest! However, creating a free account allows you to save your high scores, earn achievements, and compete on our global leaderboards.

What should I do if a game isn't loading?

First, try refreshing your browser page. If the issue persists, clearing your browser cache or trying a different browser usually solves the problem. Ensure your browser supports HTML5.
